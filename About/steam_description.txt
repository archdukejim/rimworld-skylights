Skylights: powerless natural daylight for roofed rooms — glass roof panes that grow crops indoors, pass real sunlight to Biotech genes, dome skylights, grand atrium glass ceilings, and stained-glass mosaic ship windows for Odyssey gravships. No electricity, no heat, day/night cycle aware.

[h1]☀️ Skylights[/h1]
Bring real daylight into a roofed room. [b]Powerless and cool[/b], skylights light a room by the actual sky above — bright at midday, fading through dusk, dark at night, dimmed by storms and eclipses. They never make a spot brighter than the day outside. The honest trade against a sun lamp: free and it never overheats a room, but it's only as bright as the day outside, and dark every night.

Two forms — [b]panes[/b] that open a roof tile to the full sky (crops + true sunlight), and [b]domes[/b] that spread a soft glow over a radius — across a full tech progression.

[h2]🏛️ Two forms, seven skylights[/h2]
[b]PANES[/b] open roof tiles to the full sky — they grow crops, and clear ones pass true sunlight to Biotech genes:
[list]
[*][b]Basic Skylight[/b] — the pre-industrial pane, hand-built from wood + a basic pane. Full-sky lighting and crops, but the weak frame needs a wall or pillar within 3 tiles — lose that support and the roof caves in and shatters it.
[*][b]Industrial Skylight[/b] — the sturdy structural-glass pane, drag-placed like floors, no support limit.
[*][b]Tinted Skylight[/b] — the same sturdy pane in UV-filtering tinted glass: lights and grows crops identically, but shades pawns from true sun (safe for sun-sensitive colonists; no benefit for sun-lovers).
[/list]
[b]DOMES[/b] spread a soft glow over a radius (never enough for crops):
[list]
[*][b]Dome Skylight[/b] — soft daylight over a small area; ideal for workshops, bedrooms and walkways. Also in [b]2×1 and 2×2[/b] footprints so the light sits dead-centre in an even-sized room, with a true-reach placement preview.
[*][b]Light Tunnel[/b] — a dome fitted to a mirror-lined reflection tube that pipes daylight down through solid overhead mountain.
[/list]

[h2]⚙️ Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dome light radius[/b] — a 1–10 slider (default 4) for how far the dome and light-tunnel glow reaches; the placement previews follow it.
[*][b]Skylight display[/b] — three states: [b]selectable[/b] (click skylights directly), [b]visible[/b] (classic click-through), [b]hidden[/b] (invisible, still working). Cycle from the HUD button (circle/check/X badges), the Skylights architect tab, or the mod menu.
[*][b]Sprite opacity[/b] — a 10–100% slider (architect tab or mod menu) that fades every skylight's glass/frame art.
[*][b]Stained-glass mute[/b] — optionally switch off the ship windows' coloured light whenever skylights are hidden.
[*][b]Inward roof-edge lighting[/b] ([i]experimental, under development[/i]) — reshapes the soft light at roof edges to bleed inward: Vanilla / Skylights-only / All roofs. Known rendering quirks near domes and walls.
[/list]

[h2]🏛️ NEW in v3: Atrium skylights[/h2]
Grand glass ceilings that open their [b]whole footprint[/b] to the sky — crops, moonlight and (clear) true sun on every tile beneath. Two styles, each in [b]2×2 to 5×5[/b], clear and tinted:
[list]
[*][b]Domed atrium[/b] — a geodesic dome of straight-cut panes: material-lean, but slow artisan work.
[*][b]Pyramid atrium[/b] — a Louvre-style lattice of identical triangular panes: standardized glass that builds fast but eats more of it.
[/list]
Costs are [b]countable[/b] — you pay exactly the panes and struts the sprite shows — and one-bill [b]atrium kits[/b] at the smelter make a whole atrium's materials at once.

[h2]🚀 NEW in v3: Ship windows (stained glass)[/h2]
Rotatable [b]1×1 leaded stained-glass tiles[/b] — six patterns × six tints — laid out like mosaics: quarter-arcs close into circles, chevrons into zigzags, diagonals into grand diamonds. Tinted glass [b]pools its colour in the room below[/b]; clear passes true sun.
[list]
[*][b]Odyssey-aware[/b] — full daylight on a planet; faint coloured starlight in orbit; airtight glazing. No Odyssey? Beautiful colony stained glass.
[*][b]The only glazing rated for gravships[/b] — ordinary skylights refuse to install over ship substructure.
[*][b]A luxury[/b] — windows on a spaceship cost real resources (2 glass + 6 frames per tile).
[/list]
