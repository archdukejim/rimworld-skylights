Skylights: powerless natural daylight for roofed rooms — glass roof panes that grow crops indoors, pass real sunlight to Biotech genes, and dome skylights that softly light workshops and bedrooms. No electricity, no heat, day/night cycle aware.

[h1]☀️ Skylights[/h1]
Bring real daylight into a roofed room. [b]Powerless and cool[/b], skylights light a room by the actual sky above — bright at midday, fading through dusk, dark at night, dimmed by storms and eclipses. They never make a spot brighter than the day outside. The honest trade against a sun lamp: free and it never overheats a room, but it's only as bright as the day outside, and dark every night.

Two forms — [b]panes[/b] that open a roof tile to the full sky (crops + true sunlight), and [b]domes[/b] that spread a soft glow over a radius — across a full tech progression.

[h2]🏛️ Two forms, seven skylights[/h2]
[b]PANES[/b] open roof tiles to the full sky — they grow crops, and clear ones pass true sunlight to Biotech genes:
[list]
[*][b]Basic Skylight[/b] — the pre-industrial pane. Hand-built on the spot from wood + a basic pane, no smelter or power. Full-sky lighting and crops, but the frame is weak: it needs a wall or pillar within 3 tiles to carry the roof — lose that support and the roof caves in and shatters the pane.
[*][b]Industrial Skylight[/b] — the sturdy structural-glass pane, drag-placed like floors, no support limit.
[*][b]Tinted Skylight[/b] — the same sturdy pane in UV-filtering tinted glass: lights and grows crops identically, but shades pawns from true sun (safe for sun-sensitive colonists; no benefit for sun-lovers).
[/list]
[b]DOMES[/b] spread a soft glow over a radius (never enough for crops):
[list]
[*][b]Dome Skylight[/b] — soft daylight over a small area; ideal for workshops, bedrooms and walkways. Hand-build the dome at a crafting spot, or cast it faster at an electric smelter. Also comes in [b]2×1 and 2×2[/b] footprints so the light sits dead-centre in an even-sized room, with a placement preview that outlines the footprint and shows the true light reach.
[*][b]Light Tunnel[/b] — a dome fitted to a mirror-lined reflection tube that pipes daylight down through solid overhead mountain.
[/list]

[h2]⚙️ Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dome light radius[/b] — a 1–10 slider (default 4) for how far the dome and light-tunnel glow reaches; the placement previews follow it.
[*][b]Hide installed skylights[/b] — hides the frame/glass sprite so roofs sit flush; the skylight keeps channeling daylight exactly the same.
[*][b]Skylight visibility button[/b] — a toggle on the bottom-right overlay row that shows/hides installed skylights in one click (same setting as the mod menu, applies instantly); a master switch here can remove the button.
[*][b]Inward roof-edge lighting[/b] ([i]experimental, under development[/i]) — reshapes the soft light at roof edges to bleed inward: Vanilla / Skylights-only / All roofs. Known rendering quirks near domes and walls.
[/list]
