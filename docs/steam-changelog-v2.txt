[h1]Skylights v2 — Tech Tiers, Gene-Aware Sunlight & More[/h1]

A big update: skylights are now a full progression from a crude tribal pane all the way up to UV-filtering tinted glass — plus real Biotech sun-gene support, a light-radius setting, and a fix that makes dome skylights track day and night properly.

[i]Safe to add to an existing save. Your existing skylights keep working — only their display names changed, not their internal IDs.[/i]

[h2]☀️ New: gene-aware sunlight[/h2]
Clear glass panes now let the tile below count as [b]real sunlight[/b] for Biotech genes, stats, and thoughts. Sun-loving xenotypes finally get their outdoor benefits indoors — and sun-sensitive pawns will burn under an open pane, exactly as if they were standing outside. Want the daylight without the sunburn? Glaze with the new [b]tinted[/b] pane, which filters the sun out for genes.
[i](Thanks to Rain on the Workshop for the request.)[/i]

[h2]🏛️ Two forms, five skylights[/h2]
[b]PANES[/b] open one roof tile to the full sky — they grow crops, and clear ones pass true sunlight to genes:
[list]
[*][b]Basic Skylight[/b] — NEW. The pre-industrial pane, unlocked at [b]Smithing[/b]. Hand-built from wood + a basic pane, no smelter or power. Full-sky lighting and crops — but the frame is weak: it needs a wall or pillar within 3 tiles, and if that support is ever lost the roof caves in and shatters the pane (the materials are destroyed).
[*][b]Industrial Skylight[/b] — the sturdy structural-glass pane (Electricity). [i]This is the former "paned skylight".[/i]
[*][b]Tinted Skylight[/b] — NEW. The same sturdy pane in UV-filtering tinted glass (Complex Furniture). Lights and grows crops identically, but blocks the sun for genes — safe for sun-sensitive colonists, no benefit for sun-lovers.
[/list]
[b]DOMES[/b] spread a soft glow over a radius (never enough for crops):
[list]
[*][b]Dome Skylight[/b] — now unlocked at [b]Smithing[/b] and [b]no longer requires a smelter[/b]: hand-build the dome at a crafting spot, or cast it faster once you have an electric smelter.
[*][b]Light Tunnel[/b] — pipes daylight down through solid overhead mountain (Electricity + Complex Furniture). [i]This is the former "mountain dome skylight".[/i]
[/list]

[h2]🔧 New items & recipes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Basic pane[/b] — NEW item. Crude glazing hand-worked at a crafting spot. 1 steel → 1 (single or x10 bulk). Needs Smithing.
[*][b]Skylight dome (by hand)[/b] — NEW recipe. 2 steel → 1 at a crafting spot, no research required.
[*][b]Tinted structural glass[/b] — NEW item. Cast at an electric smelter (Electricity + Complex Furniture).
[/list]

[h2]⚙️ New setting[/h2]
[b]Dome skylight light radius[/b] — a 1–10 slider (default 4) in Mod Settings, controlling how far the dome and light-tunnel glow reaches. Takes effect immediately.

[h2]🐛 Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Dome skylights now properly [b]track day and night[/b] — previously their brightness could stick at the level it had when installed.
[/list]

[h2]🌱 Low-tech friendly[/h2]
You no longer need Electricity to enjoy skylights — the Basic Skylight and Dome Skylight are both available at [b]Smithing[/b] and build without a smelter.
